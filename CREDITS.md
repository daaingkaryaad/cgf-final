## Adapted Course Material

The vector and matrix utilities, the initial Phong-reflection approach, and the original window and build setup were adapted from the Computer Graphics Fundamentals Assignment 4 materials. The final project extends these with the launch hierarchy, world-space lighting, additional lights, shadows, fog, camera behavior and scene integration.
